Copy

Be it hereby made known that, between the colonist Krückeberg,
No. 18 at Berenbusch, and the shoemaker and lodger Wilhelm Meÿer,
resident at No. 41 in Cammer, with the approval of the Princely
Rent Chamber, the following contract of sale has been negotiated
and concluded.

I.

The holding No. 18 at Berenbusch, which consists of a dwelling
house, a small yard, and an adjoining garden of approximately
one morgen in size, is sold by the colonist Krückeberg, under the
existing hereditary tenancy (peasant-law) conditions, to the
shoemaker and lodger Wilhelm Meyer for the purchase price of
five hundred fifty thalers in current coin.

II.

The dwelling house is sold together with everything that is
attached thereto and fixed to the walls and by nails, likewise
together with the stoves and the floorboards and posts which may
be found lying loose on the floor area by way of description.
