Most Serene Prince,
Most Gracious reigning Prince
and Lord!

Res. Cam.: To the Office, by brief note, for a report on the financial
circumstances and on the personal qualities of the petitioner.
B. 2 Oct. 1808.
P[?]kaas

Until now I have lived as an Einlieger with Wilharm No. 11 in Evesen, but
now wish to establish myself independently. Before Bärenbusch there lies a
place which had previously already been inspected by order of the Forestry
Office, on the occasion that another person wished to establish himself
there, but who could not pay for the place.

This place, however, is very inconvenient for building a house, since it
consists entirely of uneven ground, the filling and leveling of which will
require much effort. Yet it is suitable for me because it is not too far
from Lauten, a circumstance to which I must pay particular attention on
account of my profession as a tailor.

Since I now hope that Your Serene Highness, in consideration of the great
effort which the leveling of this place will require, will not set the
price of it too high, there issues to Your Serene Highness my most humble
petition:

that that place be graciously granted to me for house-building at a
reasonable price.

I remain in deepest reverence to Your Serene Highness

Your most humble servant
Tailor Krückeberg in Evesen

Petitioner.
Bückeburg, the 29th Sept.
1808.
H Wellman
