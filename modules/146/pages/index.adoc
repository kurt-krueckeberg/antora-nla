= Holding No. 18 in Berenbusch (1808-1810 & Sept. 1850)

This case file covers documents from two time periods:

. From 1808 to 1810
. Various dates in September 1849

The first time period concerns Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg's petition before
the Schaumburg-Lippe Rentkammer, which was the princely financial and property
administration office responsible for managing state revenues, domains, and
landed estates. The process lasted until 1810.

The second set of documents concern the request by Jobst Heinrich's son,
Johann Heinrich, to have the Rentkammer approved the sale of his holding at
no. 10 Berenbusch because he plans shortly to immigrate to the United
States.
