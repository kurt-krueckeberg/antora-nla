= Document 9: Draft of a Cameral decree addressed to this colonist Krückeberg

== Summary

On December 7, 1809, Jobst Heinrich's wife, Philippine Christine Eleonore, nee Tegtmeyer,
xref:146:doc8.adoc[petitioned the Rentkammer] to allow two payments instead of one for
the plot of land the Rentkammer xref:146:doc7.adoc[had decided to alloted to Jobst Heinrich],
where he would build a home and could plant a garden of crops and house a few animals.

NOTE: Payment for the tract of land, the *"erhebliches Kolonat"*, transferred
only hereditary tenancy rights to the land not full ownership; ultimate title
remained with the territorial authority.

Ref. no. 394
Concept: Chamber Decree

To the new settler Krückeberg
No. 18 at Berenbusch

Dispatched: Schwarze

The petition submitted on the 7th of this month by the new settler Krückeberg,
requesting installment-based payment for the plot of land assigned to him for
house construction, is not granted.

Bückeburg, 12 December 1809.

F. St. g.

By authority of Lindesberg
M. Kaas
