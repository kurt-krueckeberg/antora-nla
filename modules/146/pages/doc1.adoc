= Document 1: Petition submitted by the tailor Krückeberg in Evesen
:page-role: wide

[.narrow]
== Overview

This is Schuster Krückeberg's petition to purchase a tenancy holding on land
where he can build a future home that was presented to the Rentkammer on 30
September 1808. The number *1* is visible in the upper right corner of the
document bordered by one side of square.

The purchase did not grant the buyer title to the land. That remained with
the State. The buyer gained a inheritable tenacy right to the land. There
was an initial payment, then year charges and obligations.

The Rentkammer's response is written just below the petition's salutation.

[NOTE]
====
Schuster Krückeberg states that *"Ich habe bisher als Einlieger beÿ Wilharm No 11
in Evesen gewohnt (Up to now, I have been living (lodging) with the Wilharms
at no. 11 in Evesen)"*.

Einlieger here means: a person currently residing in another household without
a separate holding; a statement of present residence, not of hereditary
status or poverty.

Rentkammer petitions frame the petitioner’s present lack of independent
establishment in order to justify granting one.
====

=== Hans Heinrich Wilharm was one of Carl Friedrich (born 1807) Baptismal Sponsors

We know that when Carl Friedrich Krückeberg was
xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-B-1807a.adoc[born in 1807], one of his
xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-B-1807a.adoc[baptismal sponsors] was Hans Heinrich
Wilharm of no. 11 Evesen.

Hans Heinrich Wilharm was xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-B-1760a.adoc[born in
Berenbusch in 1760], and in 1796 he
xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-M-1796a.adoc[married Anne Marie Philippine Blecke
of no. 11 Evesen]. She died after giving birth to their son
Hans Heinrich Wilharm], who was xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-B-1797a.adoc[born
11 April 1797] at no. 11 Evesen. Later the same year, her husband 
xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-M-1797a.adoc[remarried to Sophie Ernestine
Blecke], who had been living with them already.  

The birth records of their daughter Anne Marie
Christine, xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-B-1798c.adoc[born in 1798], and their
son Carl Heinrich, xref:genealogy:petzen:PET-B-1810a.adoc[born in 1810],
show the family:

* continued to live a no. 11 Evesen
* Hans Heinrich was the Colon (Stätte holder) of no. 11 Evesen
* He worked as an Ackermann (farmer) in 1810.

No. Cam. 260.                         presented 30 Sept. 1808.

[marginal note]
(undeciphered docket mark)

Most Serene Prince,
Most Graciously Reigning Prince
and Lord!

_For ordinary handling by the Princely Rent Chamber._

Resolution of the Chamber:
To the District Office, by brief referral, for a report
on the financial circumstances and on the personal qualities
of the petitioner. 2 Oct. 1808.
P. Kraas

Up to now I have lived as a tenant with Wilharm No. 11
in Evesen, but I now wish to establish myself independently.
In front of Bärenbusch there lies a plot of land which
had previously already been inspected by the Forestry
Office, on the occasion that another person wished to
build there, but who was unable to pay for the plot.

This plot, however, is indeed very inconvenient for
house construction, since there are only uneven grounds
upon it, the filling and leveling of which will require
much effort. Nevertheless, it is suitable for me for
the reason that it is not too far removed from people,
which I must take particular account of because of my
profession as a tailor.

As I now cherish the hope that Your Serene Highness,
in consideration of the great effort which the leveling
of this plot will require, will not set the price of it
too high, there thus proceeds to Your Serene Highness
my most humble petition:

that You may most graciously transfer to me that plot
for house construction at a reasonable price.

I remain in deepest reverence
Your Serene Highness’

Most humble servant,
Tailor Krückeberg in Evesen

Sept.
Submitted on the 29th of September
1808.
H. Swellmann

=== Translation Notes

*Einlieger* →  tenant (non-landholding resident)

*mich selbst anzubauen* →  to establish myself independently (i.e., to build and maintain one’s own holding)

*Forst-Amtswegen* →  by the Forestry Office

*Gründe* →  uneven grounds / irregular ground

*Leuten* →  people (important for a tailor’s livelihood)

*gegen ein Billiges* →  at a reasonable price

*Ich erstrecke mich* →  formal closing: I remain
