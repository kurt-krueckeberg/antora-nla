== Document 1 Analysis

=== 1. Position of this document within the case file

This petition is *the initiating supplication (Supplik)* of the entire
case file.

* It *creates the file* administratively.
* Everything that follows (Amt report, valuation, land survey, approval,
allocation) exists *only because of this request*.
* The file title _"`Bauerngüter Colonien … in specie … Neubauer J. Henr.
Krückeberg Nr. 18 zu Berenbusch`"_ is a *retrospective archival
summary*, not the petitioner’s wording.

Importantly:

____
*At this stage (30 Sept. 1808), there is no "`Stätte Nr. 18`" yet in
reality.* The number *18* is applied _later_, once the colony site is
approved and fixed.
____

'''''

=== 2. Administrative metadata (top matter)

==== a. Filing and presentation

* *"`Nr. Cam. 260.`"* → Rentkammer incoming register number. This
anchors the petition in the Kammer’s chronological intake.
* *"`praes. 30. Sept. 1808`"* → Official receipt date by the Rentkammer.
This is the *administrative date*, not the date the petitioner signed
(29 Sept.).

==== b. Marginal routing note (Rescript)

____
*"`Res: Cam: Dem Amte brevi manu zum Berichte …`"*
____

This is _crucial procedurally_:

* The Rentkammer *does not decide immediately*.
* Instead, it orders the *local Amt (Bückeburg Amt)*:
** to report on *Vermögens-Umstände* (financial means)
** and *persönliche Eigenschaften* (conduct, reputation, suitability)
* "`**brevi manu**`" = expedited, informal administrative route.

This tells us:

* Krückeberg is treated as a *potential Neubauer*, not automatically
qualified.
* His *economic viability and moral standing* are decisive criteria.

The initials and date (2 Oct. 1808) show *rapid processing*—only two
days later.

____
The four-letter marginal marks you note are *pure filing/routing signs*
and *do not encode content meaning*. You are correct to treat them as
semantically irrelevant.
____

'''''

=== 3. Identity and legal status of the petitioner

==== a. Social status

He styles himself:

* *"`Schneider Krückenberg in Evesen`"*
* signs *"`Unterthänigster Knecht`"*

This indicates:

* *No landholding status* at present
* He is an *Einlieger* (confirmed in the text)
* Professionally a *tailor*, not an agricultural colonist by trade

This is significant: He is asking for *transition in status* — from
_Einlieger_ → _Neubauer_.

==== b. Residence

* Lived *as Einlieger bei Wilharm Nr. 11 in Evesen*
* Petition is filed *from Evesen*, not Berenbusch

This matters administratively:

* He is *not yet part of the Berenbusch community*
* The Amt must assess an *incoming settler*, not an internal
redistribution

'''''

=== 4. Description of the land requested

This is one of the most revealing parts of the petition.

==== a. Prior inspection

____
"`… der vorher schon von Forst-Amtswegen besichtiget worden …`"
____

This tells us:

* The land is *forest-origin land (Forstgrund)*
* Already *administratively identified as colonization-capable*
* Not raw wilderness, but _pre-screened surplus or marginal land_

This aligns perfectly with the file cover phrase:

____
*"`vor Forstgrunde`"*
____

==== b. Physical condition

He emphasizes repeatedly:

* uneven ground
* requires *Ausfüllung und Ebenung*
* *"`viele Mühe`"*

This is *strategic rhetoric*, not idle description:

* He is *pre-arguing for a reduced purchase price*
* This anticipates later valuation disputes

==== c. Location logic (very important)

____
"`… weil er nicht zu weit von Lauten entfernt ist, worauf ich wegen
meiner Schneider Profession besonders Bedacht nehmen muß.`"
____

This is key:

* "`Lauten`" = *people / clientele*, not a village name
* He depends on *walkable or short-distance customer access*
* He is *not positioning himself as a full-time farmer*

Administratively, this explains:

* Why a tailor is requesting a *colony house + garden*, not farmland
* Why proximity matters more than soil quality

'''''

=== 5. Nature of the request (legal formulation)

The formal request is extremely precise:

____
*"`mir jenen Platz zum Hausbau gnädigst gegen ein Billiges zu
überlassen.`"*
____

He asks for:

* *Überlassung* (grant / allocation)
* *zum Hausbau* (house + garden colony)
* *gegen ein Billiges* (at a modest / fair price)

He is *not* asking:

* for a hereditary farm
* for free land
* for arable acreage

This matches the later classification:

____
*Neubauer-Colonie*, not Bauerngut in the traditional sense.
____

'''''

=== 6. Dating, endorsements, and signatures

* *Signed:* 29 Sept. 1808
* *Received:* 30 Sept. 1808

The names at the end (e.g. *Sepp*, *H. Swellman*) are *clerical or
presentational attestations*, not co-petitioners. They typically
indicate:

* scribe
* presenter
* or intermediary who forwarded the petition

They do *not* change authorship or intent.

'''''

=== 7. What this document establishes definitively

This petition establishes:

[arabic]
. *Initiation of the case*
. Krückeberg’s *status as Einlieger + tailor*
. His *intent to become a Neubauer*
. The *specific site* (future Stätte Nr. 18)
. The land’s *forest origin and marginal quality*
. The *administrative pathway*: Rentkammer → Amt report → further action

'''''

=== 8. What it does _not_ yet establish

At this stage, it does *not* establish:

* Ownership
* Approval
* Final pricing
* Assignment number (Nr. 18 comes later)
* Legal tenure terms
* Construction permission

All of that depends on the *Amt’s report* ordered in the marginal
rescript.

'''''
