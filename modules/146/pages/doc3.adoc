Ref. No. 287

Princely Chancery rescript
to the Office of Bückeburg

concerning the petition of the tailor
Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg in Evesen

Issued.

In response to your report of the 21st of this month
concerning the proposed new building, we hereby inform you
that the assignment of the plot in question, measuring
approximately one-half to three-quarters of a Morgen, within
the boundaries indicated by the Forestry Administration to
Chief Forester Falkmann, has been approved, subject to the
following conditions:

1) that no substantiated objections arise on the part of the
holders of hude (pasturage) rights; and

2) that the petitioner demonstrate assets of at least
150 Reichsthaler.

The land and ground are to be paid for at a rate of
6 Reichsthaler per Morgen.

You are to communicate this decision to the petitioner.

As written above.
Bückeburg, 31 October 1808

On behalf of [??]

Danckwirtz   Windt
