= Narrative Timeline

== 8 July 1824

The widow Krückeberg is needy and facing financial difficulties. Doctor and pharmacies costs for her
husban's lifelong illness have reduced her to poverty. She is 58 years old and she can’t perform the
feudal duties of the Burgrest labor days and the forestry services owed to the state of a "colonist"
(farmstead holder) nor can she afford to hire someone in her place. She petitions the Rentkammer
for these duties to be remitted.
