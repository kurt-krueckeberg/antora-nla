=== Terms

[width="100%",cols="6%,94%",options="header",frame=none,gird=none]
|===
|Term |Meaning

|*Bauerngüter* |"`Farm estates`" or "`peasant farms.`" Agricultural
holdings possessed and worked by individual farmers (as opposed to noble
or state estates). These farms could be hereditary but still carried
certain obligations to the local lord or princely administration.

|*Colonien* |"`Colonies`" or "`settlements.`" In the 18th–19th-century
German context, this referred to small farm settlements newly
established by "`colonists`" or tenants on cleared land under princely
or manorial supervision.

|*ACTA* |Latin for "`records`" or "`files.`" Used as a heading on
official case files meaning "`the documents concerning [this matter].`"

|*in specie* |Latin for "`in kind`" or "`in its particular form.`" Here
it signals that the file concerns a specific physical property or case
rather than an abstract category.

|*Stätte* |Literally "`site,`" "`holding,`" or "`farmstead.`" A single
rural property that formed one unit of tenancy and obligation within a
village. In this document, _Stätte No. 10 zu Berenbusch_ identifies the
specific farm involved.
|===
