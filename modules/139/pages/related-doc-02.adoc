Petition of Colon Krückeberg,
No. 10 in Berenbusch,
requesting permission to sell his homestead.
Subject:

The purchase deed transmitted on the 8th of this month
concerning the above-mentioned matter
is hereby confirmed, returned herewith,
and the Office is hereby certified
to have received twelve Mariengroschen
as stamp penalty.
Bückeburg, 20 September 1849.
For the record,
[Signed] Spreng

Likewise:
twelve Burgfesten
and
five harvest days.
[Signed] Rinne
