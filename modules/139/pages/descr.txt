I have uploaded an image from a 20 September 1849 Scahumburg-Lippe Rentcammer document.
It is very "busy", that is, there are lots of initials and other
unfamiliar notations on it. It will attempt to describe the various
blocks of text and then give my transliterations of this areas or blocks
of text. After reading the description below, examine the image and
proofread the transliterations given below in AsciiDoc literal blocks.
Here are now those descriptions and literal blocks.

First, in the top center of the document are two document identifiers
and in the upper left is a financial charge. Here is the transliteration
as an AsciiDoc literal block:

....

Stpl. 3 mg.                      N.P.1429.
....
