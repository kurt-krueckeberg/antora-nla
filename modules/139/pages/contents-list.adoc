= Contents List
:page-role: wide

The *Designatio Actorum* shown below is the table of contents for the case file. All the documents
listed in it are from 1822 and concern the petition of the needy widow Krückeberg who petitions the
Rentcammer for relief from the obligations of manual labor required of her as a property owner. 

The case file also has several documents from 1849, when the property was sold, that are filed after
the 1822 documents, but these were not recorded in the table of contents.
