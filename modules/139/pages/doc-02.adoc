N.C. 2816.          filed
No. pr. 698.

re:
Farmstead No. 10 in
Bärenbusch.

In response to the Most Gracious Rescript of the 4th and
of the present month’s 7th, we most humbly report
that the widow Krückeberg, tenant of the referenced
farmstead, still possesses the parcel of land near the
Söhren which her late husband purchased from the
Princely Chamber for 800 rixthalers. A portion of it
she has leased out.
Bückeburg, 7 August 1824.

Sander

=== Comments of Document Identifiers

The document identifiers were written by:

* *"`N.C. 2816`"* — written by the _Amt_ clerk, repeating the
Rentkammer’s file number.
* *"`Nr. pr. 698`"* — the _Amt_’s own internal protocol number for this
outgoing report.
* Both written in the _Amt_ hand (Sander’s or his clerk’s).
* The *top-left "`Nr. 2935`"* and *"`Præs. 8 Aug 1824`"* were then added
_after receipt_ by the Rentkammer registry.
