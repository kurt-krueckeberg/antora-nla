Copy.
Stamp 1 Groschen.

Done at Bückeburg, at the Amt,
on 21 August 1849.

At the hearing appointed for today,
the Colon (farmstead holder) Krückeberg No. 10 from
Berenbusch and the Einlieger (landless cottager) Wilhelm
Bornemann at No. 14 in Nordholz appeared before
the Amt, and the former, upon the question put,
made the following statement:

To my farmstead, which I have sold for 700 Reichsthaler
to the Einlieger Wilhelm Bornemann,
belong: a dwelling house, a bakehouse, and about
1 3/4 Morgen of garden land,
and likewise yard space.

There are attached to my farmstead the following
services and dues:

12 Handburgfesten (manual and communal labor obligations),
5 harvest days,
6 days of woodcutting labor,
26 Mariengroschen, 5 Pfennige in Amts dues,
2 Mariengroschen, 6 Pfennige contribution pro simplo,
5 eggs and 5 Pfennige offering money to the
parish in Petzen,
one smoke-hen,
4 Pfennige watchman’s fee,
land services, night watch, village services, and
(said).
