The widow Krückeberg, No. 10 in Bärenbusch, declares:

Her husband died two years ago, and since during his lifetime he was almost
continually ill, she has, through the many expenses for doctor and
apothecary, become nearly destitute.

Now the performance of the Burgrest labor days and the forestry services
owed from her holding is very difficult for her, since, being now
fifty-eight years old and no longer able to perform them herself, she must
have them done by others for money. But she can no longer manage to raise
the money for the fulfillment of the Burgrest and forestry duties, and
therefore she is compelled to trouble the Princely Rent Chamber with the
most humble request to graciously remit to her, for some years, the
Burgrest and forestry labor services owed from her holding.

G. Schultze

[NOTE]
====
* *Burgrest-Handtage* -> compulsory labor days (feudal service obligations) owed
  to the princely administration connected to castle or estate maintenance.
* *Forstdienste* -> required forestry labor (woodcutting, hauling, planting).

* *“Burgrest-Handtage”*
+
_Burgrest_ basically means the “residual” labour owed to the castle or the territorial
lord by a tenant (in this case the widow at Site No 10).
+
_Handtage_ literally “hand-days”, meaning days of manual labour rather than cash payment.
+
Together: “labour-days still owed to the castle/estate”. +
→ So in your document she is saying: she is required to perform or arrange
someone to perform certain manual work for the domain (castle estate) because of
the tenure of her holding.

* *“Forstdienste”*
+
_Forst_ means forest (the lord’s woods).
+
_Dienste_ means services/obligations.
+
So: *“forest service duties”.* +
-> These are manual jobs associated with the forest—cutting or hauling wood,
clearing or maintaining forest land, maybe hauling timber for the estate—jobs
owed by the tenant to the lord’s forest administration.

* *behelligen mit der unterthänigsten Bitte* -> formulaic expression meaning
  *“to trouble [the authority] with the most humble petition.”*
====

Because of the several years of severe illness of the petitioner’s late
