== Markings that can appear on documents

[arabic]
. *Document type & parties* _Vorstellung / Gesuch / Bericht / Official
Order (Rescr./"Official Order of the Chamber")_; who to/from.
. *Dates (all of them)* Body date, *"pr. in Cam"* (presented in
Chamber), countersign dates, and any *expedition date* on slips.
. *Numbers/marks*
* *Nr pr.* (protocol number) and where it sits on the leaf.
* *N.C. ####* at the top (if present): note the number and date → check
if it’s a *running Chamber docket* (does it rise with later dates?).
* Any *former/older identifiers* on covers (for later concordance).
. *Enclosure signals*
* *Anb.* on register line or leaf.
* Phrases like *"anbei zurückerfolgende Bittschrift"*, *"Beilage/Anlage/Abschrift"*, *"Beil. A/B/…"*.
* Name the enclosed item (e.g., _Situations-Riß_, judgment copy).
. *Right-column "Topic note" alignment* Confirm the right column’s
short note (loan amount, pledge condition, sale request) matches the
body content.
. *Decision language* Look for formulas: *"findet keine Statt"* (does
not take place/denied), *"gestattet werden solle"* (shall be
permitted), *"zu bescheiden"* (to decide), *"Höchstes Rescr."*
(Highest Official Order). Capture the *exact wording*.
. *Security/collateral details* Any mention of *"gegen Verpfändung …
sämtlicher Ländereien"*, acreage, insurance value, existing debts,
valuation—these explain conditions for the loan.
. *Transmission chain* Endorsements/cover slips: *"Copt/Cpt."*,
routing (to Amt, to officer Creuzinger), "Expedirt" notes, initials.
. *Scribal notes* *Conc. [name]* (Concipist/drafter), *m[anu]
p[ropria]*; signatures of officials (Hauck, Dr. Neuhsel, Höcker, etc.).
. *Marginalia & foliation* Later pencil folios vs. ink notes; keep
separate from 1841 hands.
