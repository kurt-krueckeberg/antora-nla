order to petition the Fürst, the Prince, to be allowed to repay the debt to Hempel and Sons in installment
payments. He evidently did this after learning of the decree by the Bückeburg Amt against him, dated 13 July 1841,
that he pay "principal of 133 Reichsthaler 7 Mariengroschen, interest at 5 percent from 9 April 1833, and a fee of 4
Mariengroschen."

=== Document 2 Summary

Document 2 is the report or Bericht of the Amt Bückeburg giving their opinion on Krückeberg petition for
installment payments. The report explains: 

1. The Amt Bückeberg confirms that Hempel and Sons sued Krückeberg for the "amount of 133 rthl
7 Mariengroschen plus 5% from 1833 for a debt that stems from his parents.
2. Hempel und Söhne initially sued in 1836, and since then they "had conducted proceedings twice in appellate session and once in
supplicatory session and have thereby incurred considerable costs"; therefore
3. They would not agree to the installment payments Krückeberg had petitioned (ate the Rentcammer) to be granted by
the Prince.

protocolled as Nr pr. 1274, and sent "`b.m. zum Amtsbericht`" to Amt

* *RC Rescript (14 Oct 1841)*: The Rentkammer’s *official order* based
on the Amt’s report. "`RC`" here = *Rentkammer-Rescript* (the Chamber’s

is to decide/respond to Krückeberg as proposed.`"

👉 In short: *Document 2 is the Amt’s report, received and protocolled

== 3. Clarifications of terms

* *NC number (e.g. NC 4381, NC 4487)* = *Chamber docket/expedition
number*. Assigned sequentially as the Rentkammer logged items through
the year. Multiple NC numbers can appear in one case, because each
distinct act (petition, report, order) received its own NC.
* *RC* = shorthand for *Rentkammer*. When used with a note like _"`RC
Rescr.`"_ it means *Rentkammer-Rescript*, i.e. the Chamber’s formal
directive or decision on how to proceed.
* *Nr pr. / Num pr.* = *Protocoll-Nummer*, the unique entry in the
Rentkammer’s protocol register. This is the _contemporaneous registry
number_ for the case item. "`Nr pr.`" and "`Num pr.`" are just spelling
variants.
* *"`RC comment`"* = A margin note from the Rentkammer clerk or
councillor, directing the next step (e.g. forward to Amt, draft a
rescript).
* *"`ad NC.`"* = "`in reference to NC ####`" — explicitly linking this
document to the earlier NC number of the petition or related act.

'''''

* _Document 1_ = Petition of Krückeberg, NC 4381, protocol 1274,
presented 2 Oct, forwarded to Amt Bückeburg.
* _Document 2_ = Report of Amt Bückeburg, NC 4487, also under protocol
1274 (same case), replying "`ad NC 4381.`" Received 11 Oct, rescript
issued 14 Oct.

Together, they show the early flow of the case: petition → Amt’s report
→ Rentkammer’s rescript.
