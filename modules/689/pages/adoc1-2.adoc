The Document 2 text is a report that explains: 
1. The Amt Bückeberg confirms that Hempel and Sons sued Krückeberg for the "amount of 133 rthl
7 Mariengroschen plus 5% from 1833 for a debt that stems from his parents.
2. Hempel und Söhne initially sued in 1836, and since then they "had conducted proceedings twice in appellate session and once in
supplicatory session and have thereby incurred considerable costs"; therefore
3. They would not agree to the installment payments Krückeberg had petitioned (ate the Rentcammer) to be granted by
the Prince.
