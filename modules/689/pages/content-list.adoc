.Carifications
****
* *#3*--The *"Acten Designation (right)"* column has "gegen" (in "gegen Verpfändung seiner sämmtlichen
Ländereien"). It does not mean "against", its typically meaning, but rather "on condition of". The Rentcammer was
willing to consider a loan but only against the pledge of his entire holding. The Chamber’s willingness to approve
a loan was conditional upon his pledging all of his landholdings as collateral. 

* *#7*--*aus* in *Cpt. K. Res. aus Amt Bückeburg* is incorrect. The actual document has *an* not *aus*: *Concept
an das Amt Bückeburg*, bears no *pr. in Cam.* (incoming-presented) mark, and carries the Kammer closing *Fürstl:
p.*. *aus* is retained in the transliteration for accuracy, but rendered as *to the Amt Bückeburg* in the English
translation, noting the likely clerical slip.

* *#10*--is *not a memo*; it’s the *Obligation (the sealed promissory note itself)*. Originals of *promissory notes*
were often *kept in a separate “obligations” series* and, after repayment, *returned to the debtor*—so they
commonly *don’t carry* the file’s boxed corner number and may not be physically in the case file anymore.  _That’s
why #10 has no boxed number in the case file._
****
