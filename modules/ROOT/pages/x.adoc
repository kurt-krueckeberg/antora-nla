== RentKammer Documents: NC’s Meaning Versus Cpt/Concept Meaning and Examples

* *"`NC`" (or "`N.C.`")* marks *Rentkammer _registry / control notes_*
written *after* a document was received. These are the *instructions*,
routing decisions, approvals, or follow-up orders entered *in the
Rentkammer itself*. They are almost always *dated and signed* by the
clerk (e.g., _Reck_, _Spreng_, etc.). → Think: *"`What to do next with
this case.`"*
* *"`Cpt`" / "`Concept`"* marks a *draft text* that was *prepared to be
sent out* (an _outgoing_ letter / order / rescript / decree), but *not
yet finalized*. It is the *working draft* that will later be copied
clean and sent officially. → Think: *"`Draft of what we will send
out.`"*

=== *How they function in the workflow*

[width="100%",cols="20%,17%,16%,8%,39%",options="header",]
|===
|Stage in the Process |Marking Used |Meaning |Who Writes It |Purpose
|*After receiving an incoming request/report* |*N.C.* (Numerus
Cammeralis) + short notes |Internal instructions, routing, decisions
|Rentkammer clerk |To decide what action should be taken next (e.g.,
request info, prepare rescript, approve, reject).

|*Preparing an outgoing order or reply* |*Cpt* or *Concept* |A *draft*
of what will be sent outward |Same or another clerk |To create the text
of the reply/rescript before copying into the official registry and
sending.
|===

=== *Example (simplified reconstruction of typical sequence)*

[arabic]
. *Col. Krückeberg submits a petition* to the Amt → The *Amt* forwards
it to the *Rentkammer*.
. The Rentkammer *receives it*. On the top margin or reverse, clerk
writes: *N.C. 5399* — and below a short *instruction*: _"`Refer to Amt
for valuation and proof of necessity; report back.`"_ *Signed/dated.*
. The Rentkammer receives the Amt’s report back → *another N.C.
instruction* is added: _"`Draft the rescript permitting sale under
condition X.`"_
. A *Cpt / Concept* is now written — this is the *draft text* of the
actual *rescript* that will be sent to the Amt.
. Once finalized, the *clean official copy* is written out, signed by
the appropriate authority, and *sent*. The *Concept* often remains
attached in the file.

=== *Does "`Cpt / Concept`" appear only on certain types of documents?*

Yes:

* It appears *only* when the Rentkammer is *issuing an outgoing
communication* (a *Rescript*, an *Order*, a *Decree*, a *Request for
further information*, etc.).
* You *will not* see "`Cpt`" on petitions, Amt reports, contracts, or
receipts — unless some clerk *reused the back of those sheets* to draft
something (which occasionally happens).

If there is *no outgoing action* required (for instance, a simple filing
of proof), there will be *N.C. notes only* and no Concept.

=== *In one sentence*

* *N.C.* = *Internal control and decision notes* written _on incoming
documents_ to decide *what happens next*.
* *Cpt / Concept* = *Draft of an outgoing Rentkammer communication* that
results _from those NC decisions_.

=== Rentkammer Header Examples

Here’s a plain-language explanation of the three headers and how to
understand them:

==== *Header 1:*

....
Cam: Nr: 662.
Res Cam
zum Forstamts Bericht.
praes: 30. May 1798.
B. 14. Jun. 1798
....

This header refers to a separate case numbered *662* in the records of
the *Cammer* (a central administrative or financial office).

* *"`Res Cam`"* means this is a response from the Cammer.
* *"`zum Forstamts Bericht`"* tells us the response was regarding a
report submitted by the *Forestry Office*.
* *"`praes: 30. May 1798`"* is the date the Forestry Office submitted
its report.
* *"`B. 14. Jun. 1798`"* shows the Cammer issued its response on June
14, 1798, in Bückeburg.

So this is a dated response to a forestry-related matter and unrelated
to the other two headers.

==== *Header 2:*

....
pr: 6. Merz 1799
Cam: N: 53.
2 1.
Forst-Sachen
....

This marks the registration of a new forestry-related case:

* *"`pr:`"* likely means "`protocolliert`" (registered) or
"`presentiert`" (presented), with the date: *March 6, 1799*.
* *"`Cam: N: 53`"* means this is *case number 53* in the Cammer’s
internal records.
* *"`2 1`"* could be an internal filing code—possibly bundle 2, document
1.
* *"`Forst-Sachen`"* simply means "`Forestry Matters,`" the category of
the issue.

This header introduces the case involving *Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg*
and his request to expand his property.

==== *Header 3:*

....
ad nr: Cam: 53. S. S.
Concept Cammer: Rescript
an die Beamten zu Buckeburg
....

This header is tied to the previous one—it refers to *the same case,
number 53*:

* *"`ad nr:`"* is Latin for "`concerning number`"—so this means:
"`Regarding case number Cam: 53`".
* *"`S. S.`"* is Latin for _"`supra scripta`"_—"`as written above`" or
"`see earlier.`"
* *"`Concept Cammer: Rescript`"* means this is a *draft* of a rescript
(official reply or directive) issued by the Cammer.
* *"`an die Beamten zu Bückeburg`"* shows that the directive was sent to
local officials in Bückeburg.

==== Summary:

* Header 1 (Cam: 662) is from an earlier, unrelated case in 1798.
* Headers 2 and 3 (Cam: 53) both concern the *same case* from 1799 about
Krückeberg’s petition.
* They reflect different steps in the case: first the registration of
the matter, and then the official response issued to the local Amts.
