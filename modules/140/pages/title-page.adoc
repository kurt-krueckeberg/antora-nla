= List of all tenant farmers in the districts of Bückeburg and Arensburg, with numbering of all houses. (1747)
:page-role: wide

== Overview

This document has identifier
link:https://www.arcinsys.niedersachsen.de/arcinsys/detailAction?detailid=v4869389[NLA
BU, L 101a, A Nr. 140] in the online
link:https://www.arcinsys.niedersachsen.de/arcinsys/start.action[Arcinsys
system] of Niedersachsen and Bremem, and its denotation is
_Namensverzeichnis sämtlicher Kolonen der Ämter Bückeburg und Arensburg,
mit Nummerierung der sämtlichen Häuser_, which translates _List of names of
all Kolons of the offices of Bückeburg and Arensburg, with numbering of the
all houses._  

Dr. Möhle found, however, that the year 1747 is covered.

The microfiche image below was obtained by commissioned research done by
link:https://www.moehle-research.de/[Dr. Slyvia Möhle]. It has been
enhanced as best as can be from its virtually unreadable orignal (in which
the text was whitish-yellow).
