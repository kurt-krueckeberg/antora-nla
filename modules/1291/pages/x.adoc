Draft Chamber Decree

To the Amt of Bückeburg
Subject: Land assignments

In response to the report of the 30th of last month, accompanied by a
survey drawing and concerning the above matter, the Amt is hereby informed
that the land assignments mentioned in the cited report—for Krückeberg no.
10 in Berenbusch, for Vogt no. 13 in Nordholz, and for Brüggemann no. 14 at
the same place—are approved. The Amt is to require the petitioners to pay
the sums due for these land assignments.

Furthermore, the Amt is advised that the dues (Praestanda) are to be paid
by the petitioners after the expiration of three free years, that is, no
later than Michaelmas 1822.

Accordingly, the Amt is to take the necessary measures and to notify the
parties interested in the land assignments.

Bückeburg, 17 June 1818.

By princely authority, etc.

Spring
