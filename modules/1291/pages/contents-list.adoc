== Glossary of Terms

[%header,cols="2,5",frame=none,width=70%"]
|===
|Term (as in source) |Meaning / Explanation

|Designatio Actorum
|Register or index of documents in a case file.

|Vorstellung
|Formal petition or submission to an authority.

|Conc. K. Rescript
|Finalized chancery rescript, i.e. a written decision issued by the chancery.

|Res. Cam.
|Resolution of the Chamber (Rentkammer).

|Einlieger
|Lodger or non-holding resident without an independent farmstead.

|Neubauer
|New settler granted a newly established holding.

|Kammercasse
|Chamber treasury or fiscal office.

|Ausweisung
|Allocation or assignment of land by an authority.

|Forstgrund
|Forest land under administrative control.

|Specification
|Detailed list or itemized statement attached to a report.
|===
