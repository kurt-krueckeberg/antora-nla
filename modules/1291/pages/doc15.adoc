St. 3 mg.

To the Neubauer Brüggemann No. 14
in Nordholz

vdanckwarts
Wind
MKaas
Dispatched by: Knöttge.

The request of the Neubauer Brüggemann that the plot
assigned to him for house construction be granted to him
free of charge cannot be granted; rather, payment for it
is to be made immediately.

Bückeburg, 7 Oct. 1818.

By order of the Prince
