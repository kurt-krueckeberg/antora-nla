[set of initials]

C.

Ans Amt Buckeberg Ausweisung

Das Amt wird beauftragt, den Krückeberg N. 10 zu Berenbusch wissen, daß ihn
die auf die Anlage erzeichnete Forstade, ist ohngefähr einen halben Morgen
haltend, gegen billige Bezahlung ansgewiesen werde solle.

Zuvor hat aber d. Amt über die etwaigen Widersprüche der Hutberechtigten,
nach deren Vernehmung, zu berichten.


Bbg. 13 Feb

[set of initials]

Exp: pr: Schultz

.Note on the Size of a Morgen
****
[NOTE]
====
The Morgen was a traditional German unit of land measurement whose exact
size varied by region. In Schaumburg-Lippe in the early 19th century, one
Morgen generally corresponded to approximately 0.25 hectares (about 0.6
acres, or roughly 6,000 square meters). Accordingly, the half-Morgen
referenced in this document would have measured about 0.12–0.13 hectares
(approximately 0.3 acres, or about 3,000 square meters). Because the Morgen
was not a standardized unit, these figures represent approximate modern
equivalents.
====
****

N.C. 62                              3

C.

To the Amt of Bückeburg
Land Allotment

The Amt is instructed to inform Krückeberg No. 10 at Berenbusch that the
forest parcel marked on the plan, being approximately one half Morgen in
size, is to be allotted to him in return for reasonable payment.

However, before doing so, the Amt is to report on any possible objections
of the holders of pasture rights, after hearing them.

Bückeburg, 13 February

Expedited by Schultz
