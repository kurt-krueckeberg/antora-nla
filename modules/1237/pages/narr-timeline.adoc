== 1. The Petition is Initiated at the Amt (29 May 1798)

On May 29, 1798, _Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg_, a Colonist at No. 10 in _Berenbusch_,
petitioned the _Amt in Bückeburg_ to purchase adjacent plots to enlarge his garden and
farmyard. An official named _Lindemann_ recorded his request.
This identifies Lindemann as the scribe of the petition, acting on Krückeberg’s behalf.

== 3. Forestry Assessment by MKaas (14 June 1798)

Upon reviewing the petition, the Cammer requested a _Forstamt_, the forestry authority, report likely to ensure
that the land Krückeberg requested would not unduly affect timber resources or regulated forest land.

A Foresty official named _MKaas_ wrote the report on 14 June 1798, which appears
directly below the left-side header of Document 1.

MKaas concluded that:

- The area in question was near the village and not part of any important forest tract,
- The land could be cleared, but three saplings should be planted elsewhere as compensation.

== 4. Mariginal Note of Brief Written Order to Amt Bückeburg (18 February 1799)

On 18 February 1799 a marginal note (written on the Amt's Petition) by Forstmeister
Kaas records that a brief written order from the Vormundschaftliche Kammer was sent
to the Bückeburg Amt, instructing it to report on the case. The exact wording of that
order and any enclosures (such as the Forestry Report) are not preserved; only this
short reference and the Amt’s subsequent xref:contents-list.adoc#desfallsiger-bericht[Desfallsiger
Bericht] in the Designatio Actorum (Document No. 2) remain.
