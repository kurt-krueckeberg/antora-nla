= Administrative Filing Note (rubric) 

== Summary

This brief cover note summarizes Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg’s petition to
acquire land adjacent to his garden. It likely served as a forwarding cover
when the document was submitted to the Vormundschaftliche Kammer, the
princely fiscal authority responsible for managing domains, finances, and
inheritance matters in Schaumburg-Lippe.
